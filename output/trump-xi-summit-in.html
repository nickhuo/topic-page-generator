Key Outcomes

Trump and Xi agreed to build a "constructive, strategically stable relationship" and pledged to avoid the "Thucydides Trap"—the historical pattern of conflict between rising and ruling powers. Xi committed that "China's door to opening up will only open wider," signaling economic engagement.
