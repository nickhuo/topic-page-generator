Background

OpenAI released GPT-5.5 Instant on May 5, 2026, introducing a new default model for ChatGPT. The release marked a significant update to the platform's capabilities, with the model becoming the default option for users. This launch came as part of OpenAI's broader efforts to enhance its AI offerings and maintain competitiveness in the rapidly evolving artificial intelligence market. [1][2]

Beyond the consumer-facing release, OpenAI also deployed specialized versions of GPT-5.5 for cybersecurity applications. In early May 2026, the company rolled out GPT-5.5 Cyber to vetted cybersecurity teams, positioning it as a response to Anthropic's Mythos model. OpenAI had previously provided GPT-5.5 to the U.S. government for national security testing, demonstrating the model's potential applications in critical infrastructure protection. [3][5][6]
